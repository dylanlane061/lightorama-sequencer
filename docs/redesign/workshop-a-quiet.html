The workshop look, quieter

This is the toymaker's bench direction worked into the real website and the app's two main screens, using the site's real wording. Wood stays in two places: the bar along the top and the frame around the show. Everything else is paper, painted metal and brass. It's still a mockup: the lights animate, but the buttons don't do anything.

Compared with the first pass: no texture behind the text, the heavy headline face only on the big headings, the typewriter face only on times, one workshop prop per screen instead of one per section, and nothing tilted.

Chases that match
