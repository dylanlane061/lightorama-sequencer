Three workshop looks for Papa Russ Light Co.

Each board shows the same two screens: the top of the website, and the app's "Open a show" screen. They're mockups, so the buttons don't do anything yet. Pick the one that feels most like Papa Russ, or tell me which pieces to mix.

Why the current site feels like every other site

  • Small all-caps labels above every heading ("HOW IT WORKS", "WHAT IT CAN DO"). Most generated pages have them.
  • One gold word in a white headline ("Watch your show before the neighbors do").
  • Matching rounded cards and a near-black navy page, the standard SaaS kit.
  • Inter-style sans and monospace labels, with nothing that could only belong to a Christmas light show.

The string of bulbs under the hero is the one piece with real personality, and all three directions build on it.
