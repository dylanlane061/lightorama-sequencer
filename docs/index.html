How it works

You direct. The assistant does the clicking.

Open a show you already have, or ask the assistant for a new one. Either way you watch it, say what you'd change, and get the next version back.

  1. Open your show

    Drop your .loredit into the app, with the song. Add the .lorprev layout if the sequence doesn't include it.

  2. Watch and leave notes

    Press N at any moment to pause and say what you saw. Click lights on the house to tag the props you mean."Snowflakes should spin like train wheels here."

  3. Send to your assistant

    One click turns your notes into a message for ChatGPT, Claude or Codex with the add-on, which fixes each one safely.

  4. Open the new version

    You get a new .loredit for S6 and a preview. Open it in the app and repeat until it's showtime.

What it can do

Made for real displays, pixels and all.

It works with your own layout, whatever mix of pixel props and regular lights you have.

In the app

Plays any S6 show

Your sequence, over your layout, with your music. No S6 and no install: it runs in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Notes, right on the show

Pause anywhere, write what you saw and click lights to tag props. Notes stay with each version.

Props and solo

Find any prop, ring it on the house, or solo it to see only its lights while the show plays.

Private and offline

Shows are read in your browser and never uploaded. Recent shows reopen in one click, and the offline file works without internet.

With the AI assistant add-on

Hears the music

Finds the beats, bars, big hits and section changes, so lights land on the music instead of near it.

Pixel art on the mega tree

Draws little animated scenes at your tree's exact size: a steam whistle, a snowman, a mug of cocoa filling up.

Chases that travel together

Rooflines and verticals move in the same direction across the house, even when they're wired in opposite directions.

Safe with your files

Every file is checked before it's saved, and a backup is kept. Your original sequence is never changed.

The AI assistant add-on

Let the AI build and fix shows.

Give it your layout, a song and a few sentences, and it builds a whole show. Or send it your notes from the app and it fixes each one. It works with ChatGPT, Claude and Codex. The full guide is in ONBOARDING.md.

Download the add-on

ChatGPT

In the desktop app, open Skills in the sidebar and add the zip. Or attach the zip in a chat and ask ChatGPT to install it as a skill.

Claude

Unzip it and copy the lightorama-sequencer folder into %USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\.

Codex

Ask Codex to install the zip with its skill installer, or put the folder in your skills folder.
