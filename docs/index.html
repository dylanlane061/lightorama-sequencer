Papa Russ's Light Show Sequencer

An AI show builder for Light-O-Rama S6. Give it your layout, a song and a few sentences about what you want. It hands back a sequence ready for S6, plus a preview you can watch in any browser.

Download the latest version See it on GitHub

How it works

You stay the director. The assistant does the clicking: it reads your layout, listens to the song, and places every effect.

  1. Export your layout

    In S6, open Previews and click Export to save a .lorprev file. That's how the assistant learns where every light is.

  2. Describe the show

    Hand over the layout, the song and a few sentences: "snowflakes spin like train wheels, the tree fills like a cup of cocoa."

  3. Watch the preview

    You get a .loredit sequence and a preview of the whole show with the music. Pause anywhere and leave a note.

  4. Ask for changes

    Your notes go back to the assistant, which fixes each one and builds the next version. Repeat until it's showtime.

What it can do

It works with your own layout, whatever mix of pixel props and regular lights you have.

Hears the music

Finds the beats, bars, big hits and section changes, so lights land on the music instead of near it.

Pixel art on the mega tree

Draws little animated scenes at your tree's exact size: a steam whistle, a snowman, a mug of cocoa filling up.

Chases that travel together

Rooflines and verticals move in the same direction across the house, even when they're wired in opposite directions.

A preview you can share

One file with the music built in. It opens in Chrome or Edge, with no S6 and no internet needed.

Notes, right on the show

Press N at any moment to pause and say what you saw. Click lights on the house to tag which props you mean.

Safe with your files

Every file is checked before it's saved, and a backup is kept. The assistant never edits S6's files by hand.

Regular lights too

Floods, bulb strings and white trees get fades, twinkles and beat-keeping spirals alongside the pixels.

Getting it into your assistant

Download the zip first, then follow the steps for the assistant you use. The full guide, with a prompt to test it, is in ONBOARDING.md.

ChatGPT

In the desktop app, open Skills in the sidebar and add the zip. Or attach the zip in a chat and ask ChatGPT to install it as a skill.

Claude

Unzip it and copy the lightorama-sequencer folder into %USERPROFILE%\.claude\skills\.

Codex

Ask Codex to install the zip with its skill installer, or put the folder in your skills folder.

Then try: Use the lightorama-sequencer skill. Run its self-test and tell me if everything passed.
