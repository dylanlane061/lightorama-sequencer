The first release of the show builder.

### Build a show
- Give it your S6 layout, a song and a few sentences of direction; it hands back a `.loredit` sequence for S6.
- Finds the song's beats, bars, big hits and section changes, so effects land on the music.
- Works with pixel props, groups and regular lights (fades, twinkles, beat-keeping chases).

### Pixel art and motion
- Animated pixel-art scenes drawn at your mega tree's exact size.
- Train-track chases: groups of lights with gaps between them travel the trim together, and speed up or slow down without jumping.
- Snowflake "wheels" that spin in step across every flake.

### Preview and review
- A preview of the whole show with the music, in one file that opens in any browser.
- Press N to pause anywhere and leave a note; click lights to tag props. Notes can be copied or downloaded to send back.

### Safety
- Every file is checked before saving, with a backup kept. The assistant never edits S6 files by hand.
