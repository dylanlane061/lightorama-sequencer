Better shows out of the box: the tree pictures and the creative rules from the Polar Express show are now part of the skill.

### Pixel art for the mega tree
- New `lor_pixelart.py` with 16 ready-made scenes timed to your song: boiler, steam whistle, drive wheel, snowfall, snowman, ticket punch, present, train climb, steam stop, lantern, cocoa mug, flames, fireworks, bell and goodnight taillight.
- Works for any tree or tall grid size (`--size`), and `sheet` makes a preview strip to check a scene before placing it.
- New guide, `references/pixel_art.md`: how the tree's canvas works, why the top looks squeezed, and what makes a picture land.

### The show style, written down
- New `references/creative_direction.md`: plan the show as a beat sheet first, give every movement a purpose, keep things moving together, train-track chases on the trim, beat-keeping white trees and bushes, quiet sections that slow down instead of going dark, and traditional colours.
- The skill now reads these before planning a show and puts a picture on the tree in nearly every scene.

### Checks
- The self-test now renders a pixel-art scene too.
